# Endurance and scenario victory rules

## Endurance

Move, Search, and Strike are Major Actions. A formation displays its progress on a three-action track. After its third Major Action, the track resets to zero and Endurance degrades one state, to a maximum of Critical. The state at the start of an Action determines that Action's penalties.

- **Ready:** no state penalty.
- **Extended:** High Tempo marks Friction; Recover takes +1 Time.
- **Critical:** Move is reduced by one; Heavy Salvo is unavailable; Move, Search, Strike, Patrol, and Support mark Friction because they are complex Actions.

Replenishment at a valid logistics region improves Endurance one step and resets the Major Action track as part of its service package.

## Meridian Veil victory at T16

Each side evaluates the following operational objectives at Time 16:

- **Control — 6 points:** sole control of the central operational objective.
- **Transit — 3 points:** at least one combat-capable naval formation reaches that side's opposing approach region.
- **Escort — 3 points:** a combat-capable naval formation is within two hexes of that side's combat-capable carrier.
- **Denial — 2 points:** no enemy combat-capable naval formation is within two hexes of the central objective.
- **Withdrawal — 2 points:** the carrier remains below Heavy damage, or a Heavy but still combat-capable carrier occupies its side's withdrawal region. A Crippled or Destroyed carrier fails this objective.

A formation is combat-capable when it is neither Crippled nor Destroyed. One-sided elimination of all combat-capable formations is an immediate operational defeat. Otherwise, higher operational points win. Equal scores are resolved by more combat-capable formations, then lower aggregate damage burden, then the closest surviving naval formation to the central objective. If all measures remain equal, the operation is contested.

Damage and destruction do not award points. They matter only through objective eligibility, one-sided elimination, and the ordered capability tie-breaks.
