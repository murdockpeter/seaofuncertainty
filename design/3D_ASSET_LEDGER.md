# 3D MVP Asset Ledger

All current 3D geometry, materials, markers, effects, and geographic shapes are temporary prototype assets generated procedurally in Unity or authored as project code. They are safe to replace without changing logical IDs or hex coordinates.

| Asset family | Convention | MVP source/status | Production requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Models | `mdl_<domain>_<role>_<variant>` | Procedural Unity primitives | Licensed or commissioned model; retain author/license record |
| Materials | `mat_<theater>_<surface>_<variant>` | Project-authored materials | Record shader, texture sources, and modification rights |
| Textures | `tex_<theater>_<subject>_<size>` | None required by command map | Keep source URL/file, license, author, generation prompt, and date |
| VFX | `vfx_<action>_<variant>` | Procedural lines/rings | Avoid real-world identification leakage |
| Audio | `sfx_<action>_<variant>` | Placeholder UI audio only | Licensed master and attribution entry required |
| Geography | `geo_<area>_<layer>_<version>` | Luzon presentation uses a locally cropped Natural Earth 1:10m polygon mesh; logical terrain remains hex data | Preserve dataset/version, processing notes, attribution, and “not for navigation” notice |

Geographic baseline: Natural Earth public-domain coastline; GEBCO gridded bathymetry categories with acknowledgement. The Northern Gateway coastline is generated by `tools/build-luzon-coastline.cjs` from the local GCBH campaign cache (`global-land.geojson`), cropped to 118.5–125.8°E and 17.4–24.1°N, simplified, and packaged as 72 polygons/775 points. It is not navigational data. Generated imagery must retain its prompt, tool/model, date, seed/reference inputs when available, and replacement status beside the asset before release.
