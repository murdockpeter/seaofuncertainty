# Prototype Playtest Protocol

## Session target

Play to operational Time 16 or until one side cannot contest the Inner Sea. Use two players in pass-and-play. The current build switches the visible information picture to the side of the Ready formation.

## Record after every action

- formation, action, commitment, and next Ready Time;
- whether another choice was seriously considered;
- whether Contact Age changed the choice;
- whether the player could explain the search/combat odds;
- whether a Command Slot was desired but unavailable;
- whether the opposing player had a meaningful reaction decision;
- any rule lookup or unclear term.

## Stop conditions

Pause the test and flag the rule when:

- the same action is obviously correct three times in comparable states;
- a player cannot explain why a formation acts next;
- a Contact’s state does not imply a clear physical meaning;
- a die result feels disconnected from preparation;
- entropy is experienced only as punishment rather than as a recoverable capability problem;
- a player waits more than two opposing actions without a decision.

## First comparisons

Run paired tests changing only one switch:

1. Age-2 targeting penalty on versus off.
2. High Tempo always marks Friction versus only when Extended/Critical.
3. Endurance after three major actions versus after four.
4. Three Command Slots versus two.
5. Automatic Defend versus player-selected Reaction.

Do not tune more than one of these between paired plays. The point is to identify causal changes, not merely produce a closer game.

## Metrics worth automating next

- action frequency by type and mode;
- average delay until next activation;
- consecutive activations by side;
- Contact lifetime and number of decisions affected by Age;
- search attempts per improved/lost Contact;
- combat band distribution and damage distribution;
- command-slot occupancy over time;
- entropy source duration;
- objective presence by Time;
- player decision time.
