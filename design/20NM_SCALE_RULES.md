# Sea of Uncertainty — 20 nm MVP Scale Rulings

Status: approved prototype baseline. Rebalance after recorded playtests.

## Scale and clock

- Adjacent hex centers are 20 nautical miles apart.
- One Ready-Time point is two hours.
- A Move action costs two Ready-Time (four hours) before Friction or terrain.
- Meridian Veil ends at T16 (32 hours). Northern Gateway ends at T24 (48 hours).

## Movement

Surface groups, carrier groups, and submarines move 1/2/3 hexes (20/40/60 nm) for Cautious/Normal/High Tempo, capped by current Move rating. Air groups use mission radii of 4/6/8 hexes (80/120/160 nm); this abstracts launch, transit, on-station activity, and recovery into the four-hour action. Aircraft do not remain as continuously moving pieces between actions.

Surface and submarine formations cannot end in Land. Entering Littoral costs +1 Ready-Time (two hours). Strait and deep-water costs are normal in the MVP. Weather effects are scenario hooks until playtesting establishes useful penalties.

## Search, Strike, and Reaction

- Search: Passive 8 hexes/160 nm; Active 10/200; Focused 12/240. Select any hex within range; the Search examines that hex and all adjacent hexes (radius 1). Selecting an existing Contact centers the Search on its last-known hex. A legal Search with no detection still costs two Ready-Time and advances play normally.
- Surface Strike: Light 3/60; Standard 6/120; Heavy 9/180.
- Carrier Strike: Light 4/80; Standard 8/160; Heavy 12/240.
- Submarine Strike: Light 3/60; Standard 5/100; Heavy 7/140.
- Air mission Strike: Light 6/120; Standard 10/200; Heavy 14/280.
- Reaction/interception baseline: one hex/20 nm.

Ranges are inclusive at the boundary. Range is deterministic axial hex distance; animation and 3D offsets never alter it.

## Required playtest questions

Measure whether air missions collapse the map too quickly, whether Focused Search makes concealment irrelevant, whether Heavy Strike range dominates weapon conservation, and whether Littoral delay creates decisions rather than bookkeeping.
