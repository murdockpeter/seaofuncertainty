# Ballistic launches - SCN-BM-002

Implementation baseline: 2026-10-15. These rules cover ballistic launches. SCN-BM-003 now supplies [interception, friendly coverage, and synchronized ballistic resolution](BALLISTIC_DEFENSE_RULES.md). The four expansion scenarios and remaining product integration remain work in the master TODO. The live Meridian Veil and Northern Gateway catalog entries now explicitly equip ballistic playtest forces; compiled legacy templates and existing saves retain their original equipment. See the [playtest setup](BALLISTIC_PLAYTEST_SETUP.md).

## Capabilities and commitment

Weapon delivery is `Conventional` or `Ballistic`, independently of Light/Standard/Heavy commitment. Every formation definition/state has an optional `BallisticWeapons` profile. Range is in hexes; AttackModifier is bounded to -6..6. Capacity and Ammunition are finite nonnegative integers, with current inventory at most capacity. Zero capacity disables the capability. Profiles specify allowed Contact domains, minimum Location/Identity quality, and explicit shallow/deep submarine eligibility. A usable profile requires at least General identity, so an Unknown-domain report cannot authorize a ballistic launch.

Carrier, surface, submarine, air, logistics, and battery formations may carry an explicitly authored profile. A group profile represents the equipment within that abstraction; it does not create additional escort formations. Runtime state copies each authored profile independently.

A launch requires the active owned formation, an owned nonlost Contact present in authoritative state, an aim within that report's possible area, permitted reported domain/quality, range, ammunition, and normal Strike authorization. Crippled, Destroyed, or replenishing launchers cannot fire. Active Launcher Damage or Magazine Damage disables ballistic launches. Critical Endurance prevents Heavy commitment. All commitments spend one ballistic package; Heavy adds the existing salvo attack modifier but does not expend the conventional Heavy capability. Conventional magazines remain unchanged.

After authorization, actual occupancy/domain/depth is checked privately. Empty aims, false Contacts, stale fixes that miss, and destroyed, friendly or actually ineligible targets consume the package and action and report only **no confirmed effect**. A hidden submarine's depth never changes a precommitment legality reason. An eligible submarine hit requires the corresponding depth permission.

## Resolution and information

Launch and impact resolve in the existing Strike window. Use Effective Strike + authored ballistic modifier + salvo modifier + normal Strike Support + Contact targeting modifier. The target retains its normal effective defense, damage modifiers, and normal Reaction procedure. Defend/Evade and an explicitly played Covering Fires response retain their ordinary target-defense effects. Conventional Outer/Area/Point missile defense, automatic EW defense, naval Screen interception, and generic defensive Support do not intercept ballistic attacks. An eligible provider can now add the separate bounded interception modifier defined in the defense rules.

A committed launch makes the launcher Loud (+1 effective Signature) until its next completed action. It never creates or updates an enemy Contact automatically. Search must still locate/identify it. Attacker details go to the attacking side's log; an actual defender receives an incoming-strike result for its own target without the launcher's name or position. Reaction/evade screens use only the defender's existing report of the origin. Owned capability, ammunition and range appear in orders, inspectors and hover details. Preview highlights use reports only.

## Mobile land batteries

`BallisticMissileBattery` and Contact domain `Land` append to existing enums. Batteries deploy, reinforce, move and evade only on in-bounds, unrestricted Land. Movement follows contiguous land routes: Cautious/Normal one hex, High Tempo two, limited by effective movement and existing bonuses/penalties. Batteries cannot use conventional fire, conventional synchronized strikes, naval Patrol/Screen, or Support. They do not provide naval Control, Transit or Withdrawal presence; normal surviving combat-capability tie-breaking may include them. Search uses authored battery sensor profiles, defaulting to Passive 2 / Active 3 / Focused 4, with the ordinary signature/Contact procedure.

General battery logistics require an Airfield in a scenario logistics region at the battery's hex. Nearby fleet trains do not service land batteries. Any formation reloads ballistic ammunition only where it already has legal general logistics access and the current hex belongs to an authored logistics region with `SupportsBallisticReload = true`. Other replenishment effects remain unchanged. A noncompatible service never refills ballistic ammunition.

Battery AI selects legal owned reports, spends finite ammunition, relocates after exposure when a land route and Command Attention are available, uses compatible local service, and otherwise Recovers or Holds. It does not consult hidden enemy defenses. Owned batteries render as wheeled launchers on the public terrain surface with no naval wake; Land Contacts retain report-based markers rather than revealing the actual launcher.

## Formats and verification

Current game saves are v13, operational schema v8, authored catalogs v4, authoritative commands v3, and side snapshots v3 (SCN-BM-003 extends the original launch formats). Operation envelopes remain v4; their nested game version distinguishes the new state. Older saves/catalogs explicitly initialize zero ballistic capability and retain their conventional behavior. Legacy commands remain conventional. The shipped catalog is now v4; older v1 catalogs continue through the compatibility path; the editor upgrades v1/v2/v3 to v4 before saving. All profile lists include the appended battery kind; its conventional ranges are zero. Current authoritative state digests include ballistic profiles and inventory through the save payload. Ballistic synchronized declarations now persist delivery and declared domain. Full legacy replay compatibility and UI reaction-choice persistence remain SCN-BM-004 work.

Automated checks cover legal and rejected commitments, false/stale/empty and ineligible aims, inventory isolation, land routes, explicit reload access, launch privacy, authored IDs/profiles, legacy defaults, save round trips, authoritative commands, owned snapshots, AI choice, and production UI selection/model construction. Run focused Unit, Integration, ScenarioAuthoring and Presentation checks, then content simulation, Regression, SharedArchitecture and Windows build checks with Unity closed. Passing automated checks does not record visible Solo/Hotseat or balance acceptance.
