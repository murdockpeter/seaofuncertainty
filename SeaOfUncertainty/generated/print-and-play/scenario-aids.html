Special Rules

Fixed paired-test deployment with finite magazines and layered defense. Ballistic playtest: each submarine and the Red land battery carries two ballistic packages (surface/land targets only). Surface groups carry three interceptor packages, rating 4, covering friendly surface formations within four hexes; carriers carry one rating-2 self-defense package. Initial intelligence provides a fresh general carrier fix to each side. Existing logistics sites support separate ballistic and interceptor reloads, subject to normal logistics access. Launching raises signature; interceptions spend ammunition without consuming the ordinary Reaction. Stocks and ratings are provisional playtest values.

Victory

Score operational control, transit, escort, denial, and carrier preservation/withdrawal at T16. Damage breaks tied operational scores.

Narrows Shield

A compact six-formation carrier-defense operation. Hold the Inner Sea passage while managing finite ballistic salvos and escort coverage.

Area: Meridian Veil Archipelago · 20 nm/hex · Horizon T12 · Weather: Clear (0)

Blue: MissionCommand · Red: MissionCommand

Setup

R-CVRedCVG TempestJ6
R-SGRedSurface Action TwoI6

Special Rules

Six formations, clear weather and no reinforcements. Blue begins near the passage with Resolute covered by Valiant; Red pressures from the east with Tempest, its escort and Battery Cinder. Red has three ballistic packages; Blue Nightfall has one. Valiant has two rating-4 interceptor packages at radius four; the Red escort has one. Each carrier has one rating-2 self-defense package. Opening carrier reports are fresh General/High fixes; they can age or become stale. Hover friendly markers or pin Inspector > Show Defense Coverage. Interception is automatic and finite, separate from the ordinary Reaction. Logistics can restore conventional weapons and damage but neither ballistic nor interceptor stocks in this operation. No points are awarded directly for damage, launching or interception.

Victory

At T12 each side scores: sole control within one hex of the passage (6), a combat-capable naval escort within two hexes of its carrier (3), no enemy combat-capable naval formation within two hexes of the passage (2), and preserving its carrier below Heavy damage or withdrawing it combat-capable to its own withdrawal region (2). Blue aims to hold; Red aims to displace Blue. Both sides use these same executable objectives. Operational points decide first, then combat-capable formations, lower damage burden and proximity. Escort scoring does not require remaining interceptor ammunition.

Action Aid

Move · Search · Strike · Patrol/Screen · Support · Recover · Replenish · Hold

Resolve the lowest Ready Time first. On ties, alternate sides when possible, then prefer lower Entropy, higher Command, and stable formation ID.
