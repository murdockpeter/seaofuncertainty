Sequence of Play & Decision Aid

Use this beside the board. It compresses the expanded rules without hiding the important choices.

10-Step Sequence

  1. Advance Time.
  2. Update Information.
  3. Select Ready Formation.
  4. Choose One Action.
  5. Choose Posture / Commitment.
  6. Enemy Reacts.
  7. Resolve.
  8. Apply Consequences.
  9. Check Entropy.
  10. Schedule Next Action.

No player turns: earliest Ready Formation acts. On a cross-side tie at that Time, priority passes away from the side that acted most recently. If only one side is Ready, it may act again.

Move

Cautious 1 hex / −1 Signature. Normal 2. High Tempo 3 / +1 Signature / Friction.

Search

Select a hex in range; examine it and all six adjacent hexes. Clicking a Contact selects its last-known hex. Search + Mode + hidden Target Signature − actual Range + Support. Passive +0. Active +1 / Loud. Focused +2 / 1 Slot. No detection still costs 2 Time and advances play.

Targeting

Low −2 • Medium −1 • High 0 • Identified +1.

Strike

Light +0 • Standard +1 • Heavy +2 + Weapon Expenditure.

Reaction

Defend +1 Defense • Evade +1 Defense + move 1 after • Counterattack Light strike if capable • Hold no modifier.

Synchronization

1 Slot; declare Strike Time. First attack normal Defense; second −1; third+ −2. Three+ participants mark Friction.

Entropy

Friction = +1 Time after complex Actions. Disruption = −1 Search/Sync. Destruction = −1 offense. 0 Low; 1 Moderate; 2 High/Disrupted; 3 Critical/Disorganized. When a source is newly marked, draw from its matching Effect deck.

Command

3 Slots. Focused Search • Immediate Retask • Synchronization • Push Through • Restore Command • Force complex Action. Begin with 3 Response Cards; resolve the benefit and any printed cost when played.

20 nm Digital MVP Scale

Adjacent hex centers are 20 nautical miles apart. One Ready-Time is two hours; a normal action of Time 2 represents four hours.
