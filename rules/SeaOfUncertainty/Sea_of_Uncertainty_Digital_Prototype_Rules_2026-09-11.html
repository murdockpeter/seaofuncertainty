This book records the rules enforced by the current digital prototype. When this PDF differs from the archived v0.6 books or design notes, the running simulation is authoritative for playtesting.

Snapshot, not a promise. Numbers and procedures here are balance candidates. The document describes the build as inspected on 11 September 2026; later code changes can supersede it.

What is rules-bearing

Formation state, the authoritative hex grid, Contacts, Ready Time, card effects, scenario data, and action resolution are rules. Models, wakes, shadows, coast meshes, animated water, labels, camera perspective, and other 3D presentation are not. Use highlighted legal hexes and the action previews when presentation and perspective appear ambiguous.

Current playable frame

  • Solo vs AI: the player commands Blue; deterministic OPFOR commands Red using Red-owned information and the same action methods.
  • Local Hotseat: two commanders share one device with secure handoffs.
  • Online preview: networking infrastructure is experimental; public playable command routing remains gated. It is not a rules-complete play mode.

Source audit: Core simulation, live field manual, scenario catalog, action UI, and regression-tested behavior. The old HTML/PDF books remain an archive.

1 • Core concepts

You do not take a side-wide turn. Individual formations become Ready, act, and schedule their next opportunity. The contest is to convert incomplete information into better-timed action than the opponent.

Scale and map

Adjacent hex centers are 20 nautical miles apart. One Ready-Time point is two hours. Hex coordinates, terrain, restricted regions, objective regions, facilities, and formation positions are authoritative even when the 3D scene visually spans or overlaps their boundaries.

Ratings

Move, Search, Strike, Defense, Signature, Command, ASW, EW, and Cyber are printed data. Current effects produce effective ratings. Lower Signature is generally harder to find because a target's effective Signature is added to the searcher's detection total.

Ready sequence

  1. Find the lowest Ready Time among undestroyed, unreserved formations.
  2. If formations of both sides tie, priority passes away from the side that acted most recently.
  3. Break remaining ties by fewer marked Entropy sources, then higher effective Command, then stable formation ID.
  4. Highlight that formation. Only it may take an action, though the active side may manage eligible missions/cards and the defender may react to a Strike.
  5. After resolution, set the acting formation's next Ready Time and repeat. Advance the clock to the next formation or scheduled event.
No pass action. Use Hold to spend 1 Time, remain in place, go quiet, and clear Checklist Churn if attached.

2 • Command & missions

Command Attention slots

Each side has a pool set by command architecture. A free slot can be occupied by an immediate retask, a mission change, Focused Search, printed entropy response, or Synchronized Strike. The interface shows Free, Occupied, and Strained slots.

ArchitectureSlotsCurrent scenarios
Centralized4Northern Gateway — Red
Mission Command3Meridian Veil — both sides
Distributed2Northern Gateway — Blue

Command Strain runs from 0–6. Every two Strain marks one slot Strained (maximum three), making it unavailable. A Carrier Group with logistics access can spend a Recover action as HQ Recovery to remove up to 2 Strain.

Standing missions

Every formation has a Mission task, objective, posture, and trigger. An action matching the current task needs no immediate Command Attention. Otherwise, a normal out-of-Mission action occupies one slot until that action resolves.

Changing a Standing Mission normally occupies one slot until the formation's next action. Confused Priorities adds another slot. Communications Latency delays delivery by 1 Time; the old mission continues and occupied slots release on delivery.

TriggerAutomatic task when condition is true
Contact LocatedStrike when the side owns any usable Contact
Entropy MarkedRecover when that formation has Friction or Disruption
Logistics RequiredReplenish when the formation needs service and has logistics access
Objective ReachedPatrol when the formation occupies the operational objective
On ReadyNo conditional task change

Broken links and disorganization

Broken Link permits only the current Mission or an authorized trigger. Mission Command and Flash Order response cards provide exceptions. A formation with all three Entropy sources is Disorganized and must first Push Through to attempt one complex action. Push Through marks 1 Command Strain and is consumed by authorization.

Non-air allowance cannot exceed effective Move; Air uses its printed mode allowance. Bonuses and Navigation Drift then modify allowance, minimum 1. Legal routes remain inside the area, cannot cross restricted areas, and cannot cross Land. A naval formation may end on Land only at a Port or Anchorage. Entering an intervening Strait ends further pathfinding; a Strait can be the destination. Any Littoral hex after the starting hex adds +1 Time. One friendly formation may end in a hex; opposing formations may coexist without automatic revelation.

Under weather severity above zero, a non-air High Tempo Move adds +1 Time. Movement increases enemy tracking uncertainty for Contacts on that formation. In this snapshot, Air movement allowance bypasses effective Move penalties; weather still delays its complex actions.

Patrol / Screen

Choose a center in the formation's hex or an adjacent hex, a Defensive/Balanced/Aggressive posture, and optionally a friendly protected formation inside the radius-1 area. A Defensive screen grants +1 Defense to friendly formations in its area on eligible Strikes; this bonus is not consumed. Any posture can intercept the first enemy moving into its area. Aggressive posture gives that interception +1 Strike and makes the screener Loud. Patrol persists until its owner performs a non-Patrol action; interception availability refreshes when Patrol is established again.

Support

Choose another friendly formation within 2 hexes and one type: Strike, Search, Defense, ASW Search, or Synchronization. A valid assignment grants +1 when consumed. Only one supporter may provide the same type to the same recipient; moving out of range clears the assignment. A supporter's next non-Support action clears its assignment. Formation Spread or EW Shadow can cancel the recipient's first applicable bonus.

Recover, Hold, and Replenish

Recover discards one selected attached Friction or Disruption card. The source remains marked if another matching card remains. Destruction cannot be removed this way. Recover also clears Support blocked by Fuel Priority Conflict.

Replenish requires service need plus access: Air Groups at an airfield inside a scenario logistics region; others at a Port/Anchorage there. Any formation may instead use a friendly, combat-capable Logistics Group within 1 hex. Service improves Endurance one step, reloads every magazine, improves Damage one step, resets the three-Major-Action track, repairs one selected Destruction card, and clears Loud. It takes 3 Time, +1 with Hull Breach. The formation cannot react while service is underway.

4 • Information warfare

Contacts and uncertainty

Contacts belong to one side. They record a target reference (or a False Contact), last known hex, Location quality, Identity quality, Age, domain when known, movement uncertainty, and possibly a contradictory second position.

AttributeLevelsEffect
LocationLow / Medium / HighBase uncertainty radius 2 / 1 / 0; Strike modifier −2 / −1 / 0
IdentityUnknown / General / IdentifiedIdentified grants +1 Strike; General or better reveals domain
Age0 and upwardAdd up to 2 uncertainty rings; optional Age-2 targeting rule gives −1 at Age 2+
UNCERTAINTY RADIUS = LOCATION RADIUS + min(2, max(Age, movement uncertainty))

A contradictory report adds a second possible center. Each possible center projects the full radius, clipped to the operational area. A Strike chooses a single aim hex in this possible area; the game tests hidden occupancy only after commitment.

When Time advances, every usable Contact ages by the elapsed amount. Beginning after Age 2, each further point degrades Location by one level; degrading a Low Location Contact makes it Lost. A successful Search resets Age and movement uncertainty to 0, clears contradiction/false status for the detected real target, and restores the reported position.

Search declaration

  1. Choose Passive, Active, or Focused Search and Location or Identity priority.
  2. Choose a center in sensor range. The center and all adjacent hexes are searched (radius 1).
  3. Authorize the action. Focused also immediately occupies one slot unless Directed Telescope prepared it free.
  4. Resolve one d6 check against every enemy formation actually in the searched area and range. Resolve eligible False Contacts privately.
  5. On success, create a Low/Unknown Contact or improve the priority attribute one level; if already maximum, improve the other attribute.
SEARCH VALUE = effective Search + mode + ASW/support + spectrum + target effective Signature − range

Mode modifier: Passive +0, Active +1, Focused +2, then subtract current weather severity. Against a submarine, ASW modifier is ASW − 2 + the better of Search or ASW Support. Otherwise add Search Support. Spectrum modifier is max(0, Cyber ÷ 2) − max(0, target EW). Active Search makes the searcher Loud. A submarine using Active or Focused Search becomes Shallow.

5 • Strike & reaction

Declare a Strike

  1. Choose one of your usable Contacts and one possible aim hex.
  2. Choose an available Light, Standard, or Heavy salvo in range. Standard/Heavy versus a subsurface Contact requires Identified identity.
  3. Commit the action. An empty, stale, or false aim expends the salvo and reports “no confirmed effect” without revealing why.
  4. If a target is present, its controller chooses one legal Reaction before the roll.
  5. Calculate Attack and Defense, find a combat band, roll one d6, and apply damage.
ATTACK = effective Strike + salvo + Strike Support + Contact targeting
DEFENSE = effective Defense + Reaction + Defense Support + Screen + layered missile defense − Destruction source penalty

One round equals 20 nm. Each formation begins with finite magazines: Carrier/Surface 4 Light, 3 Standard, 1 Heavy; Submarine 3/2/1; Air 4/2/1; Logistics 1/0/0. Heavy is also blocked by Critical Endurance, Crippled damage, Magazine Damage, Launcher Damage, or legacy expended state.

Layered missile defense

Defense layers add by salvo. Carrier and Surface use Outer 1 / Area 1 / Point 1; Air uses 0/1/1; Logistics 0/0/1; Submarine 0/0/0. Light receives all layers, Standard Area + Point, Heavy Point only. A target with positive EW gains another +1.

Reactions

ReactionEffect
Defend+1 Defense.
Evade+1 Defense; after combat move to a legal hex farther from the attacker, normally 1 hex. Orderly Withdrawal permits 2.
CounterattackAfter surviving, make one Light return attack using a usable Contact. No weapon is expended for this reaction in the current implementation.

A reaction does not change Ready Time. A formation reacts once, then refreshes after completing its own action. Replenishing formations cannot react. Crippled formations may only Defend or Hold. Counterattack also requires fewer than two marked Entropy sources, no Fire Control Hit, a usable return Contact, and Light range.

Damage states are None, Light, Heavy, Crippled, Destroyed. Higher incoming damage replaces lower. Incoming damage equal to the current state escalates one step; lower damage does not worsen it. Light damage gives −1 effective Defense through the target's next completed own action. Crippled gives −1 Move, −1 Defense, and disables Heavy.

6 • Synchronized Strikes

A synchronized strike reserves multiple formations against one Contact solution and brings them onto one future Strike Time.

  1. The active leader selects 2–4 eligible friendly participants, including itself; all must be able to synchronize, fire the chosen salvo, and reach the aim.
  2. Only one synchronized strike per side may be on the timeline. Declaration occupies one Command slot until resolution and sets Strike Time to at least T+1 and no earlier than the latest participant Ready Time.
  3. Reserved participants cannot act independently. Disorganized participants must have prepared Push Through.
  4. At Strike Time, resolve against the declared aim. If the Contact was lost, abort, retask, or explicitly continue blind.
  5. The defender receives one Reaction for the entire volley. Resolve each attack in participant order; each attack after the first reduces Defense by a cumulative 1.

Defense/Screen Support and prepared reaction defense apply on the first attack. Each participant expends its salvo. A participant normally schedules +1 additional Time after resolution; Synchronization Support or Deconfliction Cell cancels that delay for its named participant.

With 3+ participants, each participant after the first marks Friction. Prior Planning exempts the first otherwise-affected participant. Aborting at Strike Time gives every participant +1 Time and marks leader Friction. Retasking at Strike Time requires all participants in range, gives each +1 Time, and marks leader Friction.

7 • Entropy & endurance

Three sources

Friction is operational delay and coordination cost. Disruption attacks information and command links. Destruction represents material loss. Each event draws and attaches a card even if that source is already marked; effects can therefore stack.

8 • Command Responses

Each side shuffles its own 24-card deck and draws 3. Hand limit is 5. Completing a Major Action advances that side's draw track; after every three, draw one if below the limit. Unawarded progress waits until room exists. Played cards go to discard; an empty draw pile is replenished by a deterministic reverse-cut of the discard pile.

Play cards during your side's planning window. Reaction preparations are the exception and can be prepared before a committed enemy attack. The game validates targets and then discards the card. All cards listed below are mechanically supported in this snapshot.

9 • Scenarios & victory

Evaluate when the scenario reaches its Horizon. A side with no combat-capable non-logistics formation is eliminated. Otherwise score all achieved objectives:

ObjectiveVPRequirement
Control6Sole control of the operational objective: at least one undestroyed, non-Crippled Carrier/Surface/Sub within 1 hex and no such enemy presence.
Transit3Combat-capable Carrier/Surface/Sub inside the side's opposing approach region.
Escort3A combat-capable Carrier exists with another combat-capable Carrier/Surface/Sub within 2 hexes.
Denial2No enemy combat-capable Carrier/Surface/Sub within 2 hexes of the objective.
Withdrawal2Carrier remains below Heavy damage; a Heavy-damaged carrier must be in its withdrawal region. Crippled, destroyed, or missing carrier fails.

Victory order

  1. Only one side eliminated
  2. Higher operational points
  3. More combat-capable non-logistics formations
  4. Lower total damage burden across all friendly formations
  5. Closest surviving operational combatant to the objective
  6. Otherwise: Operation Contested

Meridian Veil

Horizon T16; clear weather; Mission Command (3 slots) for both sides. Fictional 12×10 archipelago, objective at hex (6,5). Fixed paired deployment; initial Contact information is asymmetric. West/East Haven port and airfield hexes provide typed logistics. Transit is toward the enemy side; withdrawal is toward the owning side's two-column haven zone.

Northern Gateway

Horizon T24; maritime haze severity 1 until T12; Blue Distributed (2), Red Centralized (4). Fictional forces on a generalized 24×20 Luzon Strait map, objective at (11,9). Basco hex (11,10) provides Port/Airfield logistics. Two safety-zone hexes are restricted. At T8, Blue's SAG Relay enters from the western approach; at T12 weather clears to severity 0.

Scoring uses actual positions. Hidden enemy presence is not revealed merely to show live control. Final scoring adjudicates it.

10 • Digital command interface

Primary 3D play

The active formation has an amber ACTIVE badge and selection treatment. Hover it and right-click to open Formation Orders near the formation. Choose order categories and commitments there; targeted actions highlight legal hexes or eligible Contacts on the 3D map. Left-click the intended map object/hex to commit. Heavy salvo receives a final confirmation.

Press Tab to turn the peripheral 2D edge HUD on or off. The 3D map, active formation marker, right-click order menu, overlay card hand, event log, and modal workflows remain the primary interface. Press H for the physical card table/hand and L for the event inspector.

Appendix A • Quick reference

Action loop

  1. Confirm highlighted Ready formation.
  2. Manage mission/cards.
  3. Choose order and commitment.
  4. Select legal map target.
  5. Resolve Reaction if struck.
  6. Apply result and next Ready.
  7. Reveal/answer Entropy if drawn.

Prototype implementation notes

  • Defense currently receives both the formation's effective Destruction-card reductions and an additional universal −1 when the Destruction source is marked during Strike/interception resolution.
  • Damage Control Priority and Repair Focus currently prepare the same Destruction-suppression state despite their differing card text.
  • Counterattack does not deduct a Light magazine in the current implementation.
  • Air movement currently uses commitment allowance directly, bypassing effective Move penalties from Critical Endurance, Crippled damage, and Propulsion Damage.
  • The optional named protected formation is stored with a Screen, but current Defensive coverage applies to any friendly formation inside the screen area.
  • Map labels, visible 3D wakes, model headings, and pointing are presentation cues; they do not determine legal movement, range, facing, detection, or combat.
